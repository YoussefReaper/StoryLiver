WHAT THE WORLD KNOWS ABOUT YOU

Nobody is told what they didn't see.

The world tracks who witnessed what. Do a thing in an empty room and it stays in that room until somebody talks. Do it in the square and the story arrives ahead of you, changed by whoever carried it.

YOU WERE SEEN

The priest says nothing while you do it, which is worse. By morning the guild knows there is a ledger missing and knows whose hands were on the desk.

Four things that make it hold

Standing is behaviour

No reputation bar. The guild simply stops holding the gate for you, and you work out why.

Consequences travel

On foot, at the speed of a carter. Word from the next valley arrives late and slightly wrong.

Death isn't the end of it

Someone inherits what you built, and the world says out loud what you were to it.

A table, witnessed

Play with friends and the world tracks each of you separately. It never broadcasts what only one of you saw.
