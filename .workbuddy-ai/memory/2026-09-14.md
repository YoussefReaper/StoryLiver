## Scenario playthrough: Youssef + Gojo in Demon Slayer from the start

Built `tools/play_scenario.py` (new) — plays one exact named scenario through the
real engine with full Session Zero answers and dumps the whole transcript plus
the engine's own records. Output: `.probe/scenario.md`.

of Demon Slayer`, answers = name Youssef / entry start / swordsman / power peer

  Limitless in constraints, his four beat-keyed lines including the honored-one

- Fate spine = the source's own beats in order (Final Selection -> swamp demon

## Canon fidelity: five fixes, and a regression I introduced fixing them

The run above was itself broken. A point-by-point critique of it (Rengoku and
Shinobu are Hashira at a Final Selection they were never at; they address the
player by name; a fabricated younger brother; UMAI shouted at nothing; the whole
cast staring DOWNhill at grounds that are UP the hill) traced to five distinct
mechanisms. All five fixed; commit `0c39130`.

1. **The floor ate the entry point.** `bootstrap()`'s floor-merge fired on
   `len(characters) < 4` — and the correct curated `start` cast is exactly 3.
   It replaced them with `fallback_cast()`'s 10-name roster. Guard is now
   `if mode != "original" and not (arc and canon_seed.arc_cast(...))`. The rule
   worth remembering: **a curated record means "canon has an answer, use it";
   silence means "leave research alone", never "dump the roster in".** That is
   why `arc_cast`/`arc_places` return `[]` rather than falling back.
2. **Nothing said the cast were strangers.** World now carries `entry_point`
   (→ `worldkit.normalise` whitelist → `narrator` early-line → `sessionzero`
   brief). A cast list says who EXISTS, never who has MET whom.
3. **`CANON_PERSONA_SYSTEM`** now: *"A LINE MUST NOT INVENT A FACT ABOUT THE
   CHARACTER."*
4. **`canon_lore` Rengoku card** dropped the "calls food delicious" mannerism
   (rendered as UMAI); Senjuro recorded alive, Shinjuro retired.
5. **Mt. Sagiri geography** — `canon_seed` `start.places` now states the Final
   Selection grounds are UPHILL; village + roads out are the only things below.
   Plus a `STRUCTURE_SYSTEM` rule: "WHERE A PLACE IS, IS PART OF WHAT IT IS."

**The regression, and the lesson.** Fix #2's sibling — the same-name guard I
added to `worldkit.normalise` — keyed on the name string alone, so a world-scale
build that named a local "Person 1" in each of 8 districts collapsed **48 people
to 6**. `test_depth`'s "a whole world is 6 characters, not 10" caught it.
Lesson: a dedupe that protects an invariant must key on **identity**, not on a
display string. A double = same name AND same origin AND same home. Two people
who merely share a name are two people. Locked in by
`test_one_person_is_one_record_but_a_namesake_is_two_people` (both halves
asserted, because a fix for either one alone is the bug).
