## 1. The one bug left open — fixed

The builder kept its own town ids and hung a canon landmark's **name** on them:

```
storm_drain_alley   ->  "Mugen Train"
north_wall_walk     ->  "Swordsmith Village"
river_steps         ->  "Ubuyashiki Estate"
```

The map *looked* right — every landmark was on it — while reading as nonsense,
and the frontier could not be trusted, because the same names it keyed on were
on the town's own streets.

**Fix (code, not prompt):** a location keeps a landmark's name only when its own
id belongs to that landmark (`butterfly_mansion` named "Butterfly Mansion" *is*
the real place, and a story may be set there). Everything else gets its own name
back from its id, and the landmark it was wearing goes out to the frontier —
which is where a landmark you can walk to belongs.

```
before:  storm_drain_alley = "Mugen Train"   (town street, landmark name)
after :  storm_drain_alley = "Storm Drain Alley"  +  beyond_mugen_train = "Mugen Train" (frontier)
```

## 2. The road out was not walkable — fixed

`resolve_movement` only scanned the current location's neighbours, so a frontier
was reachable from the hub and nowhere else; the engine also gated the move on
adjacency, so it could not be bypassed. Result: *"I take the road out to the
Swordsmith Village"* moved nobody while the narration described them leaving.

**Fix:** neighbours **and** the frontier are scored together, so a specific
destination ("the Butterfly Mansion") beats a generic word it shares with a
nearer place ("the Reach Road") — and the engine lets a frontier through from
anywhere in town. Verified through the real turn pipeline, not just the resolver.

## 3. A named franchise now stays canon when the lookup is thin

Research is an enhancement and a network call. When it was off or thin, a
setting the curated roster knows by name fell through to **"original"**, which
skipped the whole canon path — source line, frontier, canon personas, chapters —
for a franchise we can name from memory. The seed cast is merged in before the
grounding brief is written, and a seed match is canon on its own.

This is the *"Tanjiro does not know his own world"* failure arrived at from the
**network side** rather than the prompt side.
