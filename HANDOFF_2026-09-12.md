## 1. The one bug left open — fixed

The builder kept its own town ids and hung a canon landmark's **name** on them:

```
storm_drain_alley   ->  "Mugen Train"
north_wall_walk     ->  "Swordsmith Village"
river_steps         ->  "Ubuyashiki Estate"
```

The map *looked* right — every landmark was on it — while reading as nonsense,
and the frontier could not be trusted, because the same names it keyed on were
on the town's own streets.

**Fix (code, not prompt):** a location keeps a landmark's name only when its own
id belongs to that landmark (`butterfly_mansion` named "Butterfly Mansion" *is*
the real place, and a story may be set there). Everything else gets its own name
back from its id, and the landmark it was wearing goes out to the frontier —
which is where a landmark you can walk to belongs.

```
before:  storm_drain_alley = "Mugen Train"   (town street, landmark name)
after :  storm_drain_alley = "Storm Drain Alley"  +  beyond_mugen_train = "Mugen Train" (frontier)
```

## 2. The road out was not walkable — fixed

`resolve_movement` only scanned the current location's neighbours, so a frontier
was reachable from the hub and nowhere else; the engine also gated the move on
adjacency, so it could not be bypassed. Result: *"I take the road out to the
Swordsmith Village"* moved nobody while the narration described them leaving.

**Fix:** neighbours **and** the frontier are scored together, so a specific
destination ("the Butterfly Mansion") beats a generic word it shares with a
nearer place ("the Reach Road") — and the engine lets a frontier through from
anywhere in town. Verified through the real turn pipeline, not just the resolver.

## 3. A named franchise now stays canon when the lookup is thin

Research is an enhancement and a network call. When it was off or thin, a
setting the curated roster knows by name fell through to **"original"**, which
skipped the whole canon path — source line, frontier, canon personas, chapters —
for a franchise we can name from memory. The seed cast is merged in before the
grounding brief is written, and a seed match is canon on its own.

This is the *"Tanjiro does not know his own world"* failure arrived at from the
**network side** rather than the prompt side.

## 4. The paths that were only unit-tested are now walked

## 5. A canon floor beneath the model personas

`_apply_canon_personas` asks the **model** for each canon character's card, and
that stays the primary path — it is richer and current. But when that call is
offline, or the model does not know a character, the character kept whatever the
builder invented from a one-line roster note. That is the reported failure:

```
Nezuko Kamado - a quiet village girl
  VOICE: Soft-spoken and kind.
  CONSTRAINTS: Is an ordinary mortal person.
```

for a character who is **mute**, **is a demon**, and **rides in a box**.

New `backend/canon_lore.py` is a curated floor for the franchises that keep
reproducing this (Demon Slayer, Hazbin Hotel, JJK, AoT, Naruto, One Piece — ~32
characters). It is a table, not a call: it cannot go stale the way a lookup can,
and it costs nothing. Order of precedence is **model > floor > builder**, field
by field, and an invented background resident is never touched.

Verified offline, a Demon Slayer build now seats the real cast with real
personas: Inosuke speaks in the **third person** under a **boar mask**, Zenitsu
is a **wailing coward awake / precise killer asleep**, Nezuko **never speaks**
and rides in the box, Muzan is **calm and courteous and monstrous underneath**.
A crossover named "Charlie" gets her **Hazbin Hotel** card (princess of Hell,
daughter of Lucifer) even though she is standing in a Demon Slayer world.

Also fixed while in here: a crossover premise was recorded as `inspired_by`
**verbatim** ("I and Charlie from Hazbin Hotel are inside the world of Demon
Slayer"), so the narrator's source line read like the player's request. It now
records the **host setting** ("Demon Slayer").

## 6. Three seam bugs the prompt dump exposed (`baed72b`)

I dumped the exact narrator/World-Master prompts a canon scene builds (spy on
`llm.complete`). Three things showed up that no test caught:

1. **The cast vanished after the opening turn.** A build seats its opening cast
   where the player arrives, but a schedule is a *daily template* — so they were
   only there for the opening phase. A Demon Slayer world read
   `CHARACTERS PRESENT: nobody` on turn one with Tanjiro, Nezuko and Charlie all
   still on the map. **This is "Charlie wasn't even there" and "Tanjiro knows
   nothing about his own verse"** — the characters existed and were never in the
   room to demonstrate it. They now stay for the rest of day one; the schedule
   takes over on day two.
2. **The World Master didn't know the setting.** Told only `WORLD: Greyfall`, it
   adjudicated a Demon Slayer world as a generic village. It now gets the same
   SOURCE line the narrator has.
3. **The laws contradicted the setting.** The law-writer saw only the world's
   name and cast, so a Demon Slayer world got *"there is no magic here. No
   spell, vision, or supernatural power is available to anyone"* — which forbids
   the breathing techniques the setting runs on. It is now told the setting.

The narrator prompt for a canon scene now reads like the Gemini system prompt:
`THE SOURCE`, then per-character voice-sheets — Nezuko *never speaks, bamboo
muzzle, rides in the box*; Tanjiro *polite, box on his back, breathing, smells
emotion*.
